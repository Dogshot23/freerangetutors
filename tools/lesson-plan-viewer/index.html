How to use this in class

Paste your lesson plan before class starts, save it locally, then open the viewer on your laptop or tablet when the lesson begins. The layout is designed to be scannable at a glance — no editing mode, no menus getting in the way.

The local save means you can close the tab and reopen it the next day without hunting for the original document.

Works well on a tablet propped at the side of the whiteboard. The text is sized to be readable from arm's length without squinting.

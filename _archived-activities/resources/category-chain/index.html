Category Chain

A shrinking-time-limit naming game — go around the group adding items to a category without repeating, until someone hesitates, repeats, or runs out of time. Nothing to prepare beyond picking a category out loud.

5 min (or stack rounds for 10+) A2–B2 Young learner / Teen / Adult Group No prep

"The genuine 5-minute filler I reach for when a lesson ends early and I don't want to just let class out. Needs literally nothing prepared."

01Pick a category

Say it out loud — anything with 15+ obvious members works. See the ready-made list below if you don't want to think one up on the spot.

02Go around the group in order

Each student names one item that hasn't been said yet. Start with a generous limit — 5 seconds to answer — so round one feels easy.

03Drop the time limit

If the group's enjoying it, tighten to 3 seconds for round two. This is where it actually gets fun — the pressure is the point.

04Someone's out (or the round restarts)

On a repeat, a hesitation past the limit, or an item that doesn't genuinely belong — your call whether that student sits out or the whole group just restarts the category. Either works; pick whichever keeps the energy up for your group.

Ready-made categories — grab one, no thinking required:
Foods Jobs Animals Things you find in a kitchen Verbs of movement (walk, run, crawl...) Countries Things that are cold Sports Things in a classroom Emotions

Works identically online — just go around the video grid in a fixed order instead of around the room. Stack 3–4 categories back to back if you need more than five minutes; each round resets the "no repeats" rule.
